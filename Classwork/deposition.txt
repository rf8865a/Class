                     DANIEL R. KOWALCZYK,
of lawful age, being first duly sworn, as hereinafter certified,
testified as follows:

                         EXAMINATION
BY MR. BRANDT:
     Q.   Good morning. Would you please state your full name for
the record?
     A.   Daniel Robert Kowalczyk.
     Q.   And your current address?
     A.   2215 Birchwood Lane, Millbrook, Ohio.
     Q.   Mr. Kowalczyk, my name is Tom Brandt. I represent
Margaret Ellsworth in this lawsuit. Have you ever had your
deposition taken before?
     A.   No.
     Q.   Let me go over a few ground rules. The court reporter is
taking down everything we say, so I need you to answer out loud.
Nods and "uh-huhs" don't come through clearly. Do you understand?
     A.   Yes.
     Q.   If you don't understand a question, tell me and I'll
rephrase it. If you answer, I'll assume you understood. Fair?
     A.   Fair.
     Q.   Is there any reason, such as medication or illness, that
would prevent you from giving accurate testimony today?
     A.   No.
     Q.   What did you do to prepare for today's deposition?
     A.   I met with my attorney.
     Q.   Without telling me what was discussed, how long did you
meet?
     A.   Maybe an hour and a half. Yesterday afternoon.
     Q.   Did you review any documents?
     A.   Some photos. And the dog warden report.
     Q.   Did you talk with anyone other than your attorney about
this deposition?
     A.   My wife, Lisa. Just that it was happening.
     Q.   Okay. How long have you lived at 2215 Birchwood Lane?
     A.   About six years. Since spring of 2020.
     Q.   Who lives there with you?
     A.   My wife, Lisa, and our two kids. They're 11 and 8.
     Q.   And do you own the home?
     A.   Yes, jointly with my wife.
     Q.   Where are you employed?
     A.   I'm a project estimator at Delmar Mechanical.
     Q.   How long have you worked there?
     A.   Nine years.
     Q.   Let's talk about your dog. You own a dog named Bruno; is
that correct?
     A.   Yes.
     Q.   What breed is Bruno?
     A.   He's a mix. The shelter said shepherd and boxer. We never
did a DNA test.
     Q.   How old is he?
     A.   He's about five now. We got him when he was around a year
and a half.
     Q.   Approximately how much does he weigh?
     A.   About 75 pounds, last time at the vet.
     Q.   Where did you get Bruno?
     A.   Millbrook County Humane Society. In 2022.
     Q.   Were you told anything about his history when you adopted
him?
     A.   They said he was surrendered by an owner who was moving.
They said he was good with kids.
     Q.   Did they tell you anything about how he behaved around
strangers?
     A.   They said he could be, I think the word was "reserved"
with new people at first.
     Q.   "Reserved." Did they tell you anything about any prior
bites?
     A.   No. Not that I remember.
     Q.   Did you receive any paperwork from the shelter?
     A.   There was an adoption packet. I don't know if we still
have it.
     Q.   Have you looked for it?
     A.   My wife looked. She couldn't find it.

               (Plaintiff's Exhibit 1 was marked for
               identification.)

BY MR. BRANDT:
     Q.   I'm handing you what's been marked Plaintiff's Exhibit 1.
Do you recognize this document?
     A.   It looks like the dog license record from the county.
     Q.   Is that your name listed as the owner?
     A.   Yes.
     Q.   And the dog listed is Bruno?
     A.   Yes.
     Q.   Was Bruno licensed with the county on the date of the
incident, March 14, 2026?
     A.   I believe so. I renew it every January.
     Q.   You'd agree with me that you are Bruno's owner?
     A.   Yes.
     Q.   And Bruno lives at your home on Birchwood Lane?
     A.   Yes.
     Q.   Now, prior to March 14, 2026, had Bruno ever bitten
anyone?
     MS. MORENO:  Objection to form. Go ahead and answer.
     A.   No. He's never bitten anybody.
     Q.   Had he ever bitten another animal?
     A.   There was a scuffle with a dog at the park once. That was
two dogs. Nobody got hurt.
     Q.   When was that?
     A.   Maybe 2023. Summer.
     Q.   Did either dog require veterinary treatment?
     A.   No.
     Q.   Prior to March 14, had Bruno ever growled at, lunged at,
or jumped on a person who wasn't a member of your family?
     A.   He barks at people walking by. Most dogs do.
     Q.   My question is a little different. Has he ever lunged at
a person?
     A.   He's pulled toward people on the leash. I wouldn't call
it lunging.
     Q.   What would you call it?
     A.   Pulling. Being excited.
     Q.   Has anyone ever complained to you about Bruno's behavior?
     A.   Not formally.
     Q.   Informally?
     A.   Our neighbor across the street mentioned once that he
barks a lot when she gets her mail.
     Q.   Who is that neighbor?
     A.   Carol Haines.
     Q.   We'll come back to that. Let's talk about your yard. Do
you have a fenced yard?
     A.   The back yard is fenced. Six-foot privacy fence on two
sides, and chain link on the side that faces the Ellsworths.
     Q.   The Ellsworths are your next-door neighbors?
     A.   Yes. To the east.
     Q.   Is there a gate in the fence?
     A.   Two. One on the west side by the garage, and one on the
east side, the chain link side.

               (Plaintiff's Exhibit 2 was marked for
               identification.)

BY MR. BRANDT:
     Q.   I'm showing you Plaintiff's Exhibit 2. Do you recognize
what's shown in this photograph?
     A.   That's the east gate. The chain link one.
     Q.   Does this photo fairly and accurately show how that gate
looked in March 2026?
     A.   Yes. Pretty much.
     Q.   What kind of latch is on that gate?
     A.   It's a gravity latch. The kind that drops down.
     Q.   Does it lock?
     A.   No. There's a spot for a padlock but we don't use one.
     Q.   Prior to March 14, had you ever had any problems with
that gate latch?
     A.   It sticks sometimes. If you don't push it all the way it
doesn't catch.
     Q.   How long had it been sticking?
     A.   I don't know. A while. Maybe since the fall.
     Q.   Fall of 2025?
     A.   Around then.
     Q.   Did you ever repair it?
     A.   I bought a new latch. I hadn't put it on yet.
     Q.   When did you buy the new latch?
     A.   I think January.
     Q.   So from January to March 14, the new latch was in your
garage, not installed?
     A.   Correct.
     Q.   Had Bruno ever gotten out through that gate before
March 14?
     MS. MORENO:  Objection. Form.
     A.   Once.
     Q.   When?
     A.   November. Last November.
     Q.   November 2025?
     A.   Yes.
     Q.   What happened?
     A.   He got out and went into the front yard. Carol across
the street saw him and texted me. I came out and got him. He was
just sniffing around.
     Q.   Did he approach anyone?
     A.   Not that I know of.
     Q.   Let's turn to March 14, 2026. That was a Saturday,
correct?
     A.   Yes.
     Q.   Where were you that morning?
     A.   Home. I was in the garage working on my son's bike.
     Q.   Who else was home?
     A.   My wife was inside. The kids were at my mother-in-law's.
     Q.   Where was Bruno?
     A.   In the back yard.
     Q.   Was he on a leash or tie-out?
     A.   No. Just loose in the yard.
     Q.   When had you last seen him before the incident?
     A.   Maybe 15, 20 minutes before. I let him out the back door
around 10:30.
     Q.   Had anyone used the east gate that morning?
     A.   I did. I took the recycling bin out through that gate
earlier.
     Q.   About what time?
     A.   Around 9:30, maybe a little after.
     Q.   When you came back through, did you check that the latch
caught?
     A.   I closed it. I thought it caught.
     Q.   Did you check?
     A.   I didn't go back and test it, no.
     Q.   How did you first learn something had happened?
     A.   I heard yelling. A woman screaming. And Bruno barking.
     Q.   What did you do?
     A.   I ran out of the garage, down the driveway, and I saw
Mrs. Ellsworth on the ground at the end of her driveway, and
Bruno was there.
     Q.   Where exactly was Bruno?
     A.   Right next to her. Like a few feet away.
     Q.   What was he doing?
     A.   Barking. He was barking at her.
     Q.   Did you see Bruno make physical contact with
Mrs. Ellsworth?
     A.   No. By the time I got there he was just standing there.
     Q.   What did you do next?
     A.   I yelled his name and grabbed his collar and pulled him
back. Then I asked her if she was okay.
     Q.   What did she say?
     A.   She was crying. She said he bit her. Her arm was
bleeding.
     Q.   Which arm?
     A.   Her left. The forearm.
     Q.   Could you see the injury?
     A.   I could see blood. Her sleeve was torn.
     Q.   Did you see anything else on the ground?
     A.   Her phone, and a bag of something. I think it was
groceries. Some stuff had spilled out.
     Q.   What did you do with Bruno?
     A.   I took him inside and put him in the laundry room. Then I
told Lisa to call 911.
     Q.   Did she?
     A.   Yes.
     Q.   Did you go back out to Mrs. Ellsworth?
     A.   Yes. I brought a towel. Her husband had come out by then.
     Q.   Did you say anything to Mrs. Ellsworth or her husband at
that point?
     A.   I said I was sorry. I said the gate must not have
latched.
     MS. MORENO:  Objection. Move to strike as nonresponsive.
     MR. BRANDT:  It was responsive. I asked what he said.
BY MR. BRANDT:
     Q.   You said, "the gate must not have latched"?
     A.   Something like that. I was upset. I was just assuming.
     Q.   Did you go look at the gate afterward?
     A.   Yes. Later.
     Q.   What did you find?
     A.   It was open. Not all the way. Maybe a foot.
     Q.   Did the paramedics arrive?
     A.   Yes. And a sheriff's deputy, and later the dog warden.
     Q.   Was Mrs. Ellsworth taken to the hospital?
     A.   She went in the ambulance, yes.

               (Plaintiff's Exhibit 3 was marked for
               identification.)

BY MR. BRANDT:
     Q.   Mr. Kowalczyk, I'm handing you Plaintiff's Exhibit 3. You
said you reviewed this report yesterday?
     A.   Yes.
     Q.   This is the dog warden's incident report. Directing your
attention to the section titled "Owner Statement." It reads,
"Owner stated dog escaped through side gate which has faulty
latch. Owner stated he has been meaning to fix latch." Did I read
that correctly?
     A.   Yes.
     Q.   Did you make that statement to the dog warden?
     A.   I told her about the latch. I don't know if those were my
exact words.
     Q.   Is the substance of it accurate?
     A.   Yes.
     Q.   And Bruno was placed under a ten-day quarantine at your
home?
     A.   Yes. He was current on his rabies vaccine.
     Q.   Did anything come of the quarantine?
     A.   No. He was fine.
     Q.   Since March 14, has the county designated Bruno as a
nuisance, dangerous, or vicious dog?
     MS. MORENO:  Objection. Calls for a legal conclusion. You
can answer if you know.
     A.   I got a letter saying he was designated as dangerous. We
appealed it.
     Q.   What's the status of that appeal?
     A.   It's still pending, I believe.
     Q.   Since the incident, what changes have you made?
     A.   I installed the new latch. And a padlock. And he only goes
out on a leash now or when someone's out there with him.

               (Plaintiff's Exhibit 4 was marked for
               identification.)

BY MR. BRANDT:
     Q.   Let's look at Plaintiff's Exhibit 4. These are text
messages produced by your counsel in discovery. Do you recognize
them?
     A.   Yes. Those are texts between me and Carol Haines.
     Q.   The first one is dated November 8, 2025, from Ms. Haines.
It says, "Your dog is out again and he's going after the mail
carrier." Did I read that correctly?
     A.   Yes.
     Q.   "Out again." Had Bruno gotten out more than once before
November 8?
     A.   No. I think she was exaggerating. That was the one time.
     Q.   And "going after the mail carrier." What did you
understand that to mean?
     MS. MORENO:  Objection. Calls for speculation.
     A.   I took it to mean he was barking at him. When I got out
there the mail truck was already down the street.
     Q.   Did you ever ask the mail carrier what happened?
     A.   No.
     Q.   Your reply, sent four minutes later, says, "Got him.
Sorry. Gate latch is junk, gotta fix it." Did you write that?
     A.   Yes.
     Q.   So as of November 8, 2025, you knew the latch was
defective and that Bruno could get out through it?
     MS. MORENO:  Objection. Form. Mischaracterizes.
     A.   I knew it stuck sometimes. Yes.
     Q.   And Mrs. Ellsworth was injured about four months later,
after Bruno got out through that same gate?
     MS. MORENO:  Same objection.
     A.   Yes.
     Q.   Prior to March 14, did you have any knowledge that
Mrs. Ellsworth had done anything to provoke, tease, or torment
Bruno?
     A.   I didn't see what happened before I got out there.
     Q.   I understand. But do you have any information, from any
source, that she did anything to provoke Bruno?
     A.   Lisa said she saw her waving her arms from the window.
     MS. MORENO:  I'll just note that Mrs. Kowalczyk will be
deposed separately.
BY MR. BRANDT:
     Q.   Other than what your wife told you, do you have any
information that Mrs. Ellsworth provoked the dog?
     A.   No.
     Q.   Would you agree that someone waving their arms while a
75-pound dog is running at them is trying to protect themselves?
     MS. MORENO:  Objection. Calls for speculation. Argumentative.
     A.   I don't know what she was doing. I wasn't there.
     Q.   Have you had any contact with Mrs. Ellsworth since the
incident?
     A.   Lisa brought over flowers and a card a few days later.
After the lawsuit was filed, our lawyer told us not to talk to
them.
     Q.   Do you have homeowner's insurance?
     A.   Yes.
     Q.   With whom?
     A.   Buckeye Mutual.
     Q.   Have you made a claim?
     A.   Yes. They're the ones who hired Ms. Moreno, I believe.
     MR. BRANDT:  I'll pass the witness for now.
     MS. MORENO:  I have a few questions.

                         EXAMINATION
BY MS. MORENO:
     Q.   Mr. Kowalczyk, in the four years you've owned Bruno, how
many people would you estimate he has interacted with?
     A.   Hundreds. Neighbors, kids' friends, birthday parties.
     Q.   Has any of those people ever reported being bitten,
scratched, or injured by Bruno before March 14, 2026?
     A.   No. Never.
     Q.   Have Bruno's veterinarians ever noted any aggression
concerns?
     MR. BRANDT:  Objection. Hearsay, foundation.
     A.   No. Not that they've told me.
     Q.   You were asked about the November 2025 text. When you
went outside that day, did you observe Bruno making any physical
contact with anyone?
     A.   No. He was in the front yard sniffing the bushes.
     Q.   Did the mail carrier or the postal service ever contact
you about Bruno?
     A.   No.
     Q.   On the morning of March 14, after you came through the
east gate with the recycling, what did you do with the gate?
     A.   I pulled it shut behind me. I heard it click.
     Q.   You heard it click?
     A.   I believe so. Yes.
     Q.   Did anyone else use that gate between 9:30 and the time
of the incident, to your knowledge?
     A.   Not to my knowledge. But I was in the garage. I couldn't
see that side.
     Q.   When you arrived at the end of the Ellsworths' driveway,
where was Mrs. Ellsworth relative to her own property line?
     A.   She was right at the end of her driveway, near the
sidewalk.
     Q.   Did you hear Mrs. Ellsworth say anything about what
happened before you arrived?
     A.   She said, "He came out of nowhere."
     MS. MORENO:  That's all I have.

                     FURTHER EXAMINATION
BY MR. BRANDT:
     Q.   Just a couple of follow-ups. You testified you heard the
gate click. Earlier you told me "I thought it caught." Which is
it?
     MS. MORENO:  Objection. Argumentative.
     A.   Both. I thought I heard it click, so I thought it caught.
     Q.   But when you checked the gate after the incident, it was
standing open about a foot?
     A.   Yes.
     Q.   And you'd already known for at least four months that
the latch didn't always catch?
     MS. MORENO:  Objection. Asked and answered.
     A.   Yes.
     Q.   And Mrs. Ellsworth saying "He came out of nowhere" --
you'd agree that's consistent with her not having seen the dog
until it reached her?
     MS. MORENO:  Objection. Calls for speculation.
     A.   I guess. I don't know.
     MR. BRANDT:  Nothing further.
     MS. MORENO:  We'll read and sign.

               (Deposition concluded at 11:22 a.m.)
